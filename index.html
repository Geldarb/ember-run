EMBER RUN

A co-op roguelite shooter. Clear rooms, grab guns, stack perks, topple the golem.

Skill
How to play

Desktop: WASD move · Mouse look · Left click shoot · Right click aim · Space jump · Shift dash · Q skill · R reload · 1/2 or wheel swap guns · E pick up / open · M mute · Esc pause.

Mobile: left thumb = move stick, drag right side = look, hold FIRE (you can drag on it to aim too), plus AIM / JUMP / DASH / SKILL / R / ⇄ buttons. Play in landscape.

Each floor is a chain of rooms. Doors seal when you walk in; beat every wave to open them and pick 1 of 3 perks. Follow the light beam. Defeat the golem at the end of each floor and step into the portal. Co-op: stand next to a downed friend for 3 seconds to revive them.
